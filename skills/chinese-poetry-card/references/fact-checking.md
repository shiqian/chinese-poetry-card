# Fact-checking reference

For every non-obvious claim, keep a working record:

claim: <fact used>
source: <work, author, volume/chapter, URL or edition>
status: primary / later record / modern scholarship / disputed
card_use: poet / background / omitted
wording: <safe wording for the image>

## Source order

Prefer the poem's reliable textual edition; primary historical works, local gazetteers, collected works, criticism, and prefaces; then university, museum, library, or scholarly research. Institutional summaries are for orientation, not decisive proof of anecdotes.

## Cautions

- Use 约 where dates differ; do not infer an exact itinerary from a later summary.
- Separate a broad genealogy from a specific traditional claim such as 宋太祖八世孙; do not present the specific claim as direct 宋史 wording without verification.
- Identify later anecdotes as later records. Omit or qualify disputed claims rather than forcing certainty into a child-facing card.
- Check names, places, official posts, collection titles, studio names, poetic styles, and quotations character by character.
