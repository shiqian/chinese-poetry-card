# Card layout reference

Use the five images in assets/yueke-style-reference/ as the canonical visual and structural reference. They establish the series rhythm and five-card division, not a character or scene template.

## Shared system

- One exact portrait 3:4 size for all five images.
- The ornate cream-and-gold card border, parchment panels, calm dark-green typography, hand-painted picture-book finish, bottom scroll, feather mark, and exact 羽言课堂 placement are fixed series identifiers. Keep them on every card, including the modern idiom card.
- Cover and first three cards share one newly designed poet identity and connected historical world.
- Idiom card uses a contemporary child-life scene and may use a gentle blue title distinction; it remains quiet, printable, and compatible.
- Secondary plants, objects, weather, and surrounding ornaments come from the new poem or child-life scene. Keep them subordinate; do not copy reference plants or props unchanged.
- Leave clear negative space behind text. Use one main title per card.

## Card compositions

- Cover: match the reference cover's title plaque, information order, illustration balance, and footer placement. Use 一诗一成语, poem title, author, related idiom, and brand only.
- 诗歌学习卡: title and [朝代]作者; central poem scene; narrower poem slip on the left and wider 诗意小课堂 panel on the right, with calm staggered edges. Meaning is plain paraphrase only.
- 诗人简介卡: one integrated panel and one 诗人简介 title. The portrait should express this poet's temperament rather than a generic poet face.
- 诗歌背景卡: title plus poem title; connected setting; a small number of separate rows or callouts for hidden context.
- 成语学习卡: idiom as main title; modern child-centered scene; 释义 and 例句 sections.
